title: JUSTIFIED summary: Line text up on the right of an alphanumeric item instead of the left. keywords: JUSTIFIED JUST RIGHT right-align alignment alphanumeric MOVE UNSTRING truncation ---

Syntax

{ JUSTIFIED | JUST } [ RIGHT ]

What it does

Normally, when you MOVE text into an alphanumeric item, it goes in from the left: short text is padded with spaces on the right, and text that is too long loses characters on the right. JUSTIFIED RIGHT turns this around for the receiving item. Short text is padded with spaces on the left, so it ends at the right edge, and text that is too long loses characters on the left.

It is allowed only on elementary alphabetic or alphanumeric items (PIC A or PIC X), not on numbers, which are always lined up by their decimal point. RIGHT is optional; JUST is short for JUSTIFIED.

JUSTIFIED affects only data moved into the item. A VALUE clause is not moved, so VALUE "AB" still starts at the left.

UNSTRING into a justified item

An UNSTRING piece that is shorter than the item is lined up on the right, as with MOVE. Remember that the piece includes any spaces it contains: when the sending field is a PIC X(12) holding DOG,EMU, the piece after the comma is EMU followed by five spaces, which fills an 8-byte item from the left. A piece that is too long is cut on the right, unlike MOVE (see the notes).

Example

"CAT" goes to the left of the plain item and to the right of the JUSTIFIED one. "ELEPHANT" is too long for PIC X(4) JUST, so the MOVE keeps the rightmost four characters, HANT. RIGHT-START shows that a VALUE is not right-justified. The first UNSTRING puts "EMU" on the right. In the last one, the second piece is the twelve characters IJKLMNOPQRST, and the justified item keeps the first eight of them.

SP COBOL notes

When an UNSTRING piece is longer than a JUSTIFIED RIGHT item, GnuCOBOL 3.2 fills the item from the left and drops the extra characters on the right, where a MOVE would drop them on the left. SP COBOL keeps this behavior on purpose so that programs give the same results as under GnuCOBOL (DECISIONS.md, "Kept as stock"). If you need the MOVE behavior, UNSTRING into a plain item and then MOVE it to the justified one.
