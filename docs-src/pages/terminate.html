title: TERMINATE summary: Finish a Report Writer report, printing its last footings and final totals. keywords: report writer end report finish FINAL grand total REPORT FOOTING ---

Syntax

TERMINATE report-name-1 ...

What it does

TERMINATE ends a Report Writer report. It prints everything that is still owed: the control footings for the last values of the control fields, the CONTROL FOOTING FINAL group (grand totals), the page footing of the last page, and the report footing. Then LINE-COUNTER goes back to 0.

TERMINATE does not close the file. CLOSE it afterwards, as usual. If you forget TERMINATE, the last subtotals and the grand total are never printed. After a TERMINATE you can INITIATE the report again.

Example

The two GENERATEs print MON and TUE. TERMINATE prints the WEEK total (the FINAL footing) and the report footing, and LINE-COUNTER is 0 afterwards. In SP COBOL, as in GnuCOBOL, the report footing goes on a page of its own: page 1 is padded to its 9 lines first, and LINE PLUS 2 puts "END OF REPORT" on line 2 of the new page.
