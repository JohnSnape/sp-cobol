    Data description

    RENAMES

    Level 66: give one name to a run of neighboring items in a record.

    Syntax

    66 data-name-1 RENAMES data-name-2 [ { THROUGH | THRU } data-name-3 ] .

    What it does

    A level-66 entry gives a new name to part of the record just above it, without changing the record. With THRU, data-name-1 covers everything from the start of data-name-2 to the end of data-name-3, as if it were a group of those items. Without THRU, it is simply another name for data-name-2.

    It is useful when you need a group that the record's layout doesn't have, such as "city, state and ZIP" out of an address, without reorganizing the record.

    Rules

    • 66 entries come right after the last item of the 01 record they belong to.
    • The renamed items must be in that record, and data-name-3 must come after data-name-2.
    • You can't rename an 01 or 77 item, a level-88 or another 66, or an item with (or inside an item with) OCCURS.
    • A RENAMES of several items acts like a group: it is alphanumeric and its length is the total of the items it covers.

    Example

    CITY-STATE-ZIP covers CITY, STATE and ZIP: 10 + 2 + 5 = 17 bytes. POSTAL-CODE is just another name for ZIP. Moving one 17-character string into CITY-STATE-ZIP fills all three items by position.
